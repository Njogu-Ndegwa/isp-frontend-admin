Pawa WiFi charges a flat monthly fee (KES 1,500 on its Starter plan) plus a one-time KES 1,500 onboarding fee. Bitwave Technologies charges [3% of hotspot revenue with a KES 500 minimum](/pricing) and no setup fee. Bitwave costs less below about KES 50,000 a month in hotspot revenue. Above that, Pawa with your own paybill costs less.

We build Bitwave, so this comparison comes from one side. Every Pawa fact below is from Pawa's own website, checked on 22 October 2026. The two products price in opposite ways, so which one is cheaper depends on how much your hotspot earns, and we show where Pawa comes out ahead. For the wider market, see our [comparison of Kenya's ISP billing systems](/blog/best-isp-billing-system-kenya-2026).

## Which is cheaper, Bitwave or Pawa?

It depends on your monthly hotspot revenue. Bitwave's fee grows with your sales. Pawa's subscription stays fixed, but adds 5% of net sales if you let Pawa collect the M-Pesa payments for you. <!-- Source: https://pawa.co.ke/support/pricing, checked 2026-10-22 -->

| Monthly hotspot revenue | Bitwave | Pawa, own paybill | Pawa collects |
|---|---|---|---|
| KES 10,000 | KES 500 | KES 1,500 | KES 2,000 |
| KES 30,000 | KES 900 | KES 1,500 | KES 3,000 |
| KES 50,000 | KES 1,500 | KES 1,500 | KES 4,000 |
| KES 100,000 | KES 3,000 | KES 1,500 | KES 6,500 |

Pawa figures use the Starter plan and treat revenue as net WiFi sales. Pawa also charges KES 1,500 once for onboarding. <!-- Source: https://pawa.co.ke/pricing, checked 2026-10-22 -->

The break-even is KES 50,000 a month. Below it, Bitwave costs less, and a new hotspot taking KES 10,000 pays a third of Pawa's price. Above it, Pawa on your own paybill costs less, and the gap widens as you grow. A large site taking KES 100,000 a month would pay KES 1,500 a month less on Pawa, provided it has its own Daraja paybill or till. If Pawa collects the payments, Bitwave is cheaper at every level in the table. You can test your own numbers on our [pricing page](/pricing).

## Who configures the MikroTik router?

Pawa's KES 1,500 onboarding covers your account, packages, payment setup and connection to your existing MikroTik network, including a router readiness check and a WireGuard link where needed. <!-- Source: https://pawa.co.ke/mikrotik-setup, checked 2026-10-22 --> A fuller router configuration service (hotspot profiles, queues, walled garden) is listed separately and priced by quote. <!-- Source: https://pawa.co.ke/products, checked 2026-10-22 -->

Bitwave configures the router for free: hotspot, login page, packages, and M-Pesa STK push with automatic activation when the payment confirms. You never write a RouterOS command, and most operators are selling in under two hours.

## Where is Pawa the better fit?

- **Large hotspots with their own paybill.** Above KES 50,000 a month, a flat KES 1,500 beats a percentage.
- **Starting from nothing.** Pawa sells MikroTik routers and access points and quotes for site surveys, cabling and Starlink installation, so you can buy the kit and the billing from one company. <!-- Source: https://pawa.co.ke/products, checked 2026-10-22 -->
- **Fibre and estate projects.** Pawa's Custom plan lists SmartOLT and fibre workflows. <!-- Source: https://pawa.co.ke/, checked 2026-10-22 -->

## Where is Bitwave the better fit?

- **New and small hotspots.** You pay KES 500 a month until 3% of your revenue passes that, and nothing up front.
- **PPPoE networks.** KES 25 per active user means 50 users cost KES 1,250, with no plan to upgrade when you add routers. See [PPPoE billing on Bitwave](/pppoe-billing-system-kenya).
- **More than two routers.** One Bitwave account manages multiple routers with uptime, management-tunnel monitoring and live usage per customer.
- **Mixed customer types.** [Hotspot vouchers](/hotspot-billing-system-kenya), PPPoE accounts and static-IP clients sit on one dashboard, and expired customers disconnect automatically.

You can collect through Bitwave, which pays out to you on a schedule (payout fees apply), or connect your own paybill or till so payments land with you directly.

## Should I choose Bitwave or Pawa?

Choose Pawa if:

- your hotspot makes more than about KES 50,000 a month and you have your own paybill or till;
- you also need routers, access points or installation and want one supplier for all of it.

Choose Bitwave if:

- your hotspot makes less than KES 50,000 a month, or you're just starting;
- you run PPPoE customers or more than two routers;
- you want no onboarding fee and free router setup.

## FAQ

**How much does Pawa WiFi cost per month?**

Pawa's Starter plan is KES 1,500 a month for up to 3,000 customers and Silver is KES 3,500 a month above that, plus a one-time KES 1,500 onboarding fee. If Pawa collects your M-Pesa payments, it adds 5% of net WiFi sales.

**Is Bitwave cheaper than Pawa?**

Below about KES 50,000 a month in hotspot revenue, yes: Bitwave charges 3% with a KES 500 minimum and no setup fee. Above that, Pawa's flat KES 1,500 Starter plan with your own paybill costs less.

**Does Pawa charge 5% on every payment?**

No. The 5% applies only when customer payments go through Pawa's own gateway. If you add your own M-Pesa PayBill or Till during onboarding, you pay the monthly subscription only.
