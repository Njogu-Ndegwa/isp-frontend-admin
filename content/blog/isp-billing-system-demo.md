## Why test an ISP billing system demo before you buy?

A demo shows you how hard the daily work will be, which a feature list can't do. Two systems can both list M-Pesa, vouchers and MikroTik support, yet one takes three taps to find a customer and the other takes ten. You only notice that difference by using them.

Switching later costs more than an hour of testing now. You'd have to re-provision your router, move your customers across and learn a new dashboard. If you're still drawing up a shortlist, start with our [comparison of ISP billing systems in Kenya](/blog/best-isp-billing-system-kenya-2026), then demo the two you like best.

## What should you test in an ISP billing system demo?

Test the seven screens you'll use most: dashboard, customers, transactions, vouchers, plans, routers and the login page. On each one, ask a question from your real working day. If you can't find the answer within a minute or so, a stressed version of you won't find it at 9 PM either.

| Screen | The question to answer | Where it is in the Bitwave demo |
|---|---|---|
| Dashboard | How much did I make today, and from which package? | Revenue, transactions, unique customers, Plan Performance |
| Customers | Who is online right now, and who has expired? | Filters for Hotspot, PPPoE, Static IP, Active, Online, Overdue |
| Transactions | Where did each M-Pesa payment go? | Monthly hotspot and PPPoE revenue, with each payment's reference |
| Vouchers | Can I see which codes are used and which are still unsold? | Available, Used and Disabled counts, with Copy and Disable on each code |
| Plans | Can I set the speed, price and time of a package? | Download and upload speed, KES price, duration, hotspot or PPPoE |
| Routers | Which site is down, and why? | Online and offline status, plus Diagnostics for ports, PPPoE and hotspot |
| Login page | Can I make the page customers see look like my business? | Portal Configuration: colour themes, header layout, welcome message |

### Run the checks on your phone too

Many operators check their business from a phone, not a laptop. Open the demo on the phone you actually use and try the same questions. Check that tables stay readable, that buttons are big enough to tap, and that you can reach customers and transactions quickly. A dashboard that only works on a wide screen will frustrate you the first time something breaks while you're out.

### Try the awkward moments, not just the happy ones

Sales demos show a good day, so go looking for the bad one. Find a router marked offline and see what the system tells you about it. Look for a customer who says they paid but has no internet, then work out where you'd check that payment. In the Bitwave demo, South-B Relay shows up as an offline router, and there's a separate Unmatched Payments page for money that didn't match a customer.

## What can't an ISP billing system demo tell you?

A demo can't prove three things: that real M-Pesa payments activate customers, that the system works on your own router, and that support answers when something goes wrong. It runs on sample data, so test these separately before you commit your customers to any system.

**Real payments.** Set up a very cheap package on your own hotspot and buy it yourself. We timed a [KSh 1 M-Pesa WiFi purchase](/blog/mpesa-wifi-hotspot) at about 30 seconds from choosing the package to getting online. Time yours, and check that the payment shows up in your dashboard.

**Your own router.** Ask how the system configures your MikroTik, and how long that takes. Our [MikroTik hotspot setup walkthrough](/blog/mikrotik-hotspot-setup-10-minutes) shows what one-command provisioning looks like on a hAP lite. Try it on a spare router first if you already have paying customers.

**Support.** Call or message the vendor outside office hours, before you've paid. The response you get as a prospect tells you a lot about the response you'd get as a customer.
