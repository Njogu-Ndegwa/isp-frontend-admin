# Google visibility baseline — 2026-10-22

Starting point for the blog-growth loop. Source: Search Console (property
`sc-domain:bitwavetechnologies.com`, data from 2026-07-14 when the property was
added, to 2026-10-20), live google.co.ke results checked 2026-10-22, and the
repo. Compare future reports against this page.

## Whole site, 2026-07-14 → 2026-10-20

- 1,140 clicks, 4,360 impressions, CTR 26%, average position 5.5.
- Weekly clicks grew from 11 (week of 13 Jul) to 177 (week of 12 Oct).
- 95% of clicks land on the homepage (1,081).
- About 830 clicks are brand searches: "bitwave technologies" (409),
  "bitwave billing system" (393), "bitwave" (24), "bitwave login" (5).
- Kenya 96% of clicks; Uganda 26 clicks; then Ghana, Cameroon, Nigeria.
- Indexed: 15 pages. Not indexed: 9, all harmless (favicons, CSS, the old
  `isp.` subdomain, redirects).

## Blog, same period

66 clicks, 3,690 impressions, CTR 1.8%. Most blog impressions are brand
searches showing a post as a sitelink. Google hides the query for 60 of the
66 clicks (long tail), so per-query numbers undercount.

| Post | Clicks | Impr. | Pos. | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| connect-smart-tv-hotspot-wifi | 44 | 1,922 | 5.3 | #1 on google.co.ke for "how to pay for wifi on smart tv" |
| best-isp-billing-system-kenya-2026 | 13 | 682 | 7.5 | real query "best isp billing system in kenya" at pos 36 |
| how-to-start-wifi-hotspot-business-kenya | 3 | 74 | 9.3 | keyword at pos 22 |
| hotspot-business-cost-kenya | 2 | 267 | 1.7 | #2 on google.co.ke, low-volume keyword |
| custom-hotspot-login-page | 2 | 19 | 4.6 | |
| mikrotik-pppoe-setup-tenda-f3 | 0 | 47 | 6.0 | |

Lesson already learned: retitling the smart TV post to the exact query ("How to
Pay for Hotspot WiFi on a Smart TV in Kenya", 2026-10-11) took blog clicks from
2–7 a week to 33 the following week. "how to pay wifi on smart tv" (85 impr.)
and "how to buy wifi for smart tv" (51 impr.) still got zero clicks at pos 5–6.

centipid has ~30 blog posts.

## What shipped in response

- Four solution pages for the buying queries (hotspot, PPPoE, M-Pesa, WiFi
  billing), linked from every footer and from every post's closing card.
- Scheduled publishing: posts dated in the future go live on that date, so a
  reviewed batch drips out without a deploy.
- "Keep reading" related posts on every article; homepage footer links to the
  three posts Google already sends readers to.
- The blog-growth loop (`.claude/skills/blog-growth-loop/`) and
  `scripts/seo/gsc-report.mjs`.
